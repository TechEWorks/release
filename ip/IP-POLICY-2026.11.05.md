# TechEWorks intellectual property and brand policy

Revision: 2026.11.05. The dated master is `IP-POLICY-2026.11.05.md` in the
TechE.Works Standards library. Changes are recorded in the shared
[licensing history](../license/CHANGELOG.md).

Copyright in TechE-authored code and documentation remains with TechE or its
identified contributors. A public software license grants only the rights it
states. Third-party code and assets retain their own ownership and license
terms. A source download, ISO or public repository does not transfer ownership
of customer data, credentials, backups or independent software.

The names TechE, TechE.Works, ServOS, WorkDesk, product logos, certification
marks and release-signing identity are TechE marks. The software license does
not grant endorsement, certification, or the right to present a modified build
as an official TechE release. Recipients may make accurate statements about
origin and modifications without implying TechE approval.

Official release status is established by a versioned record in this repository,
an exact artifact checksum, and a corresponding source snapshot. Modified builds
must identify their changes and meet the licenses of all included components.
TechE partner or managed-service terms apply only through a separate agreement;
they do not remove an independent recipient's public-license rights.

Each product release identifies its own code license, component notices,
support scope and known limitations. Report suspected misattribution or
infringing material through a private security or maintainer channel rather
than publishing credentials or customer data in an issue.

This policy is the shared TechEWorks baseline. It does not replace the actual
license text or a product-specific release record.
